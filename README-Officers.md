# RouletteBot - Officer Guide

RouletteBot runs **Exile Roulette**, a fun way for members who are bored of their build to get a random new one. One button press spins a wheel for a **class and ascendancy** and a slot reel for a **main skill**.

## How Members Use It

**🎰 Pull the Lever** — Click the button on the Exile Roulette panel. A spinning wheel and reel animation plays, then turns into a result card showing the Class, Ascendancy and Main Skill.

Results are **private**. Only the person who pulled the lever sees their spin and result, so the channel stays clean.

## Good to Know

**Cooldown** — Each member can pull once every 10 seconds.

**Fair odds** — Every ascendancy (22) and every main skill (134) has the same chance. There are no filters.

**What's left out** — Kalguuran skills, skills that only come from weapons or unique items, and buffs, curses, marks, warcries and movement skills. Every result is a skill gem you can make from an Uncut Skill Gem and build around.

**Missing or wrong skill?** — Let @Deity know and the list will be updated.

## Officer Commands

**!roulettesetup** — Posts the Exile Roulette panel in the channel you type it in, and removes the old panel. Administrator only.
